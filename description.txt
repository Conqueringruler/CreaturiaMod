Creaturia is a mod all about adding unique, interesting creatures to the game, meant to deviate from the typical content most mods would include. 

With a variety of creatures and interactions designed entirely for this mod, as well as some based on real animals and mythology, all are meant to add more memorable and unique experiences to the game.

Content Includes: 

- 32 Items
- Over 20 New Creatures!
- 5 Weapons
- 4 Minibosses!
- 4 Buffs
- 2 Unique… Talismans? 
- 2 Accessories
- 2 Incredible Music Tracks
- 1 (Very Mobile) Plant
- 1 Minion 
- 1 Ammo 
- 1 Hook
- Improved Golem progression (cool new features we promise!)
- Revamped all dye plants and a few other items to have new uses!
