[h1]Creaturia[/h1]

Creaturia introduces an assortment of unique and interesting creatures, each and every one having some kind of function unlike anything else in the game. Unlike most other content mods, there isn’t a specific focus on weapons or bosses, but instead on making a variety of unique features all meant to make the world feel much alive and interesting!


The content in this mod includes:

32 Items
Over 20 New Creatures!
6 Weapons
4 Minibosses!
4 Buffs
2 Unique… Talismans? 
2 Accessories
2 Incredible Music Tracks
1 (Very Mobile) Plant
1 Minion 
1 Ammo 
1 Hook
1 Change to Golem (cool new feature(s) we promise!)
Revamped all (non-strange) dye plants and a few other items to have new uses!

That list only includes the implemented content by the way, so all of that you can experience in a playthrough yourself!

[h3]Some Other Spoilers…[/h3]

Want to see a bit of what we mean by “Unique Content”? Here’s some examples!

[list][img]https://imgur.com/nJkrPif.png[/img][/list]

A new pre-hardmode whip, the Electric Eel has a few different unique effects! From giving enemies the electric debuff (reverse-engineered to work on NPCs), even having built-in compatibility with the effect in Calamity (as in we just have ours turn off, they win this time...), it can also charge up to increase the length of the whip, length of the effect, and strength of the hit! The whip, however, can be pretty hard to get; you can almost think of it like a summoner equivalent to the Arkahalis with its rarity of being discovered, or like the Angler’s Fuzzy Carrot with its unique requirements to get from the Fishman. 

[list][img]https://imgur.com/ScYgbuz.png[/img][/list]

The Rainbow Focus, gotten through killing the Rainbow Fish within the Hallow, lets the player heal HP for Mana. Depending on how powerful of a Mage the player is, the rainbow focus scales accordingly - 40 Mana for healing 2 HP at its lowest, to 4 HP with 100 more maximum Mana, to 6 with the Mana Flower, 8 with Magic Cuffs, etc (the final gif uses heavily combined accessories that represent multiple). 

Not only does this allow the item to scale in strength with Mage classes as they progress, it also prevents the item from becoming something any class can use to give purpose to their 200 mana (making it actually worse for Mage), something Mage-specific gear often suffers from in mods and other games. 

[list][img]https://imgur.com/u5joQ4f.png[/img][/list]

We would’ve liked for this to be a surprise, but given how many times modders (and even the amazing Relogic) have tried to fix Golem over the years, it’s important to explain what’s actually wrong with his place in progression and why this should (hopefully!) work. It’s not just because he’s too weak, it’s because there’s no incentive to do anything else but go straight to him! 

Other bosses, once the one before them is beaten, have to be tracked down far away, or need some kind of requirement to get to, and maybe even an arena built. Golem? You’re already in the jungle and have found the temple, so why not just walk over and beat him?

With what we’ve added, now there should be a great incentive. His Golem Fists now break off his body once defeated, slowly launching themselves towards the player, but with his left fist enchanted by the Pumpking, and the right by the Ice Queen, they become drastically stronger and each have unique abilities. So now, there’s more reason to explore everything Plantera did to the world other than just to get stronger; to hunt down one or both of the beings empowering Golem, to knock him down a peg before the fight. 

[img]https://imgur.com/aPUhdlV.png[/img]

In addition, weapons from the Frost Moon and Pumpkin Moon now have a significant damage boost to Golem, helping balance out the slight increase in his strength from the fists.

If you're not a fan of them, or simply have too many mods that buff Golem, they can be disabled in the config.
